About GDGC AIKTC

Welcome to GDGC AIKTC! We are a vibrant community of students passionate about Google Technologies, innovation, and collaborative learning. Join us on our journey to shape the future of technology.

Our Mission

To empower students with Google technologies by providing hands-on learning experiences, fostering innovation, and building a strong developer community that drives technological advancement.

Our Vision

To be the leading student developer community that inspires the next generation of innovators and contributes meaningfully to the global tech ecosystem through cutting-edge projects.

Our Journey

2023 - Foundation

GDGC AIKTC was established with a vision to create a thriving developer community focused on Google technologies and innovation.

First Workshops

Launched our first series of workshops covering Android development, Web technologies, and Google Cloud Platform basics.

Community Growth

Reached 100+ active members and organized multiple hackathons, tech talks, and collaborative coding sessions.

Major Achievements

Won recognition for outstanding community engagement and successfully launched mentorship programs for junior developers.

2024 - Expansion

Expanded our reach with industry partnerships, advanced workshops, and preparation for Google Developer Student Club certification.

Be Part of GDGC AIKTC

Join us today and grow your skills, network, and opportunities with Google technologies. Together, we'll build the future of innovation.
